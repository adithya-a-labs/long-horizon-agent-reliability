# Literature

The literature section distinguishes scholarly evidence from implementation observations and secondary commentary. The starting corpus is fixed to the Research Paper records in the audited Notion database; no outside paper has been added to the scholarly corpus.

## Reading paths

- [Core paper notes](core-papers/README.md): ten detailed analyses.
- [Evidence ledger](evidence-ledger.md): all 15 unique works represented by the 17 Research Paper records.
- [Study comparison](study-comparison.md): methods, environments, evidence strength, and relevance.

## Interpretation rule

A source's presence in the Notion `Research Paper` category does not determine its evidential status. Venue, method, access, and source type are assessed independently. Practitioner articles remain in the inventory but do not support substantive scientific claims.
